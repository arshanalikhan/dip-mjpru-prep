# Video lectures

Every topic of the CS-403T syllabus, in syllabus order, with YouTube lectures that explain it. Watch the short one before reading the unit notes; use the long lecture when a topic still feels shaky.

> **How to use this page:** tap a thumbnail to play it here. Each topic has a short exam-style video (5–25 min, mostly Hindi or Indian English, solved numericals) and, where one exists, a full university lecture (about 1 hour, Rich Radke, RPI). Topics marked ⭐ are the ones MJPRU keeps asking; see the [PYQ analysis](#analysis).
